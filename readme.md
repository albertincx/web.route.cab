## 🌍 The Problem & The Solution

Every morning, millions of drivers sit in congested traffic with three or four empty seats in their cars. At the same time, thousands of commuters along the very same streets struggle with crowded public transit, costly taxi fares, or exhausting transfers.

**RouteCab** bridges this gap. It is an intelligent, community-driven ride-sharing platform that connects people travelling along the **same route from Point A to Point B**. Whether you commute to work every day, travel to university, or make regular weekend trips, RouteCab turns lonely, expensive drives into shared, affordable journeys.

## ⚡ How It Works

RouteCab makes finding regular travel companions effortless:

```text
  1. Set Your Commute           2. Smart Spatial Match          3. Connect & Ride
 ┌──────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────────────┐
 │ • Pick Point A & B   │      │ • Intelligent corridor │      │ • One-click Telegram │
 │ • Select days & time │ ───► │   & radius matching    │ ───► │   direct chat        │
 │ • Set your role      │      │ • Filter by Driver /   │      │ • Agree on pickup    │
 │   (Driver/Passenger) │      │   Passenger role       │      │   and split costs    │
 └──────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └──────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Define Your Route
Drop a pin for your **Origin (Point A)** and **Destination (Point B)** using our interactive map. Specify your departure schedule, preferred travel days, and role.

### 2. Automatic Proximity Matching
Our spatial matching engine searches your route corridor. It identifies other users whose origin and destination overlap within your chosen pickup radius (e.g., within 500m to 3km), taking driving geometry and travel direction into account.

### 3. Connect Directly & Ride
View matching profiles, travel times, and mutual routes. Contact your match with a single tap via Telegram, coordinate convenient pickup spots, and split fuel costs directly—**with zero platform booking fees or middleman markups**.

- Available on any desktop, tablet, or smartphone browser.

Commuting early in the morning or late at night? Switch effortlessly between an eye-friendly **Dark Theme** and a crisp **Light Theme**. RouteCab automatically respects your device's system settings and remembers your choice.

Switch between **English** and **Russian (Русский)** on the fly with a single click—both in the web interface and within Telegram notifications.

Never miss a potential ride buddy. RouteCab monitors newly registered routes and alerts you in Telegram whenever a new driver or passenger posts a route that matches your commute.

## 🛡️ Safety, Privacy & Trust

- **Direct Community Communication**: You always talk directly with your ride partner before confirming any trip.
- **Adjustable Privacy Radius**: You never have to reveal your exact home or office address. Set your route endpoints to nearby public landmarks, metro stations, or street corners.
- **Verified Telegram Profiles**: View the user's authentic Telegram username and bio before sharing a ride.
- **No In-App Payments or Hidden Fees**: RouteCab is a peer-to-peer matching network. Any cost sharing (such as splitting fuel or toll charges) is settled mutually between driver and passenger.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><b>Is RouteCab a taxi service?</b></summary>
<p>
No. RouteCab is not an on-demand taxi or ride-hailing service like Uber. It is designed for <b>regular, recurring commutes</b> (daily work or university trips, regular weekend visits). Drivers are ordinary car owners heading to their destinations who have empty seats, not commercial taxi drivers.
</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How are trip costs calculated and paid?</b></summary>
<p>
RouteCab charges <b>zero service fees</b>. Drivers and passengers discuss and agree on fuel contribution directly (e.g., splitting fuel costs or alternating driving days).
</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need to install an app from the App Store or Google Play?</b></summary>
<p>
No! You can use RouteCab directly as a <b>Telegram Mini App</b> inside your Telegram app, or through any modern web browser at <a href="https://route.cab">route.cab</a>.
</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I change my pickup tolerance?</b></summary>
<p>
Yes! You can adjust your search radius from 200 meters up to 5 kilometers in your profile settings. Drivers can accept a larger detour for better matching, while passengers can restrict searches to walkable pickup spots.
</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What if my schedule changes?</b></summary>
<p>
You can activate, pause, edit, or delete your routes at any time from your personal dashboard.
</p>
</details>
